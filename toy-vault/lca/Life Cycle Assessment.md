# Life Cycle Assessment

Life cycle assessment (LCA) estimates the environmental effects of a product or service across
its whole life: getting raw materials, making it, using it and dealing with it at the end.

The ISO 14040 and 14044 standards describe four phases that feed back into each other:

1. Goal and scope: what question is asked, the [[Functional Unit]] and the [[System Boundary]].
2. Inventory analysis: the [[Life Cycle Inventory]] of flows in and out.
3. Impact assessment: turning flows into impact scores, see [[Impact Assessment]].
4. [[Interpretation]]: checking what the results can and cannot support.

The phases are iterative. A finding in the inventory often sends you back to change the scope.
