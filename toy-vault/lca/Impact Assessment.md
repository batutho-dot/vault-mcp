# Impact Assessment

Life cycle impact assessment (LCIA) turns the [[Life Cycle Inventory]] into a small number of
impact scores.

Steps:

1. Classification: assign each flow to the impact categories it contributes to.
2. Characterisation: multiply each flow by a characterisation factor and sum per category.
   Climate change uses [[Global Warming Potential]] as its factor.
3. Optional steps: normalisation, grouping and weighting.

Midpoint indicators stop at a physical effect such as radiative forcing. For indicators that go
further, to damage on human health or ecosystems, see [[Endpoint Indicators]].
