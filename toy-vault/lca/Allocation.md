# Allocation

When one process gives more than one useful output, its burdens must be shared out. ISO 14044
sets an order of preference:

1. Avoid allocation by dividing the process into sub-processes, or by expanding the
   [[System Boundary]] to include what the co-product replaces (system expansion).
2. Otherwise, split by an underlying physical relationship such as mass or energy content.
3. Otherwise, split by another relationship such as economic value.

The choice can change the result a lot, so it belongs in the sensitivity check of the
[[Interpretation]] phase. System expansion is closely linked to [[Consequential LCA]].
