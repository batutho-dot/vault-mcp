# System Boundary

The system boundary decides which processes are inside the study. Common shapes:

- Cradle to gate: from raw material extraction to the factory gate.
- Cradle to grave: adds use and end of life.
- Cradle to cradle: end of life feeds a new product loop.

Leaving out small flows needs a stated rule, see [[Cutoff Rules]]. Where a process makes more
than one product, the boundary choice interacts with [[Allocation]].
