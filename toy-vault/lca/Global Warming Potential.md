# Global Warming Potential

Global warming potential (GWP) compares how much heat a greenhouse gas traps over a chosen time
horizon, relative to the same mass of carbon dioxide. Results are reported in kg CO2-equivalent.

The time horizon matters: a short-lived gas such as methane looks worse over 20 years than over
100 years. Studies should state which horizon and which IPCC report the factors come from.

GWP is the characterisation factor for climate change in [[Impact Assessment|impact assessment]].
