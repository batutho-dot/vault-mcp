# Interpretation

Interpretation is the phase where results are checked before anyone relies on them:

- Completeness: is anything important missing from the [[Life Cycle Inventory]]?
- Sensitivity: does the answer flip when an assumption, such as the [[Allocation]] method,
  changes?
- Consistency: were the same rules used for every option compared?

The conclusion should say what the study supports and where it stops.
