# Life Cycle Inventory

The life cycle inventory (LCI) lists every input and output of the system, scaled to the
[[Functional Unit]]: materials, energy, emissions to air, water and soil, and waste.

Foreground data is collected for the processes the study controls. Background data, such as the
[[Electricity Grid Mix]], usually comes from a database.

The inventory is the input to [[Impact Assessment]].
