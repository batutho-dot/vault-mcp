# Functional Unit

The functional unit says what service is being compared, in measurable terms. Every flow in the
[[Life Cycle Inventory]] is scaled to it.

A good functional unit describes the function, not the product. "Lighting a room for one year
at a stated brightness" lets an LED and an incandescent bulb be compared fairly; "one bulb" does
not, because the two last for very different times.

The amount of product needed to deliver the functional unit is called the reference flow.
