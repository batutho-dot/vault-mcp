# Solar Photovoltaics

Solar photovoltaic (PV) cells turn sunlight directly into electricity through the photovoltaic
effect in a semiconductor, most often silicon.

PV gives direct current; an inverter converts it to alternating current for the grid. Output
follows the sun: zero at night, highest around midday, lower in winter at high latitudes.

In an LCA of PV, most of the climate impact sits in making the panels, so the
[[Electricity Grid Mix]] of the factory country matters.
