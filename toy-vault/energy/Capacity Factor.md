# Capacity Factor

The capacity factor is the energy a plant actually produced over a period divided by the energy
it would have produced running at rated power the whole time.

It is a ratio between 0 and 1, often written as a percentage. Weather-dependent sources such as
[[Wind Power]] and [[Solar Photovoltaics]] have lower capacity factors than plants that can run
on demand, because the wind and sun are not always available.

See [[Energy vs Power]] for why rated power alone does not say how much energy a plant gives.
