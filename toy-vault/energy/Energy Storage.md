# Energy Storage

Storage moves energy from a time of surplus to a time of need. Options differ by how long they
can hold energy:

- Batteries: hours. See [[Battery Chemistry]] for the main cell types.
- Pumped hydro: hours to days.
- Thermal storage, such as hot water tanks in [[District Heating]]: hours to weeks.
- Chemical storage through [[Power-to-X]]: weeks to seasons.

Every storage step loses some energy, measured as round-trip efficiency.
