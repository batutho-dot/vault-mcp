# Electricity Grid Mix

The grid mix is the share of each generation source in the electricity delivered by a grid.
It changes by country, by year and even by hour.

In an LCA, the mix chosen for electricity often drives the climate result of electricity-heavy
products. Two common choices:

- Average mix: the share of all sources over a period.
- Marginal mix: the source that responds when demand changes.

A grid with much [[Wind Power]] and [[Solar Photovoltaics]] has a mix that swings with the
weather, which is one reason for [[Energy Storage]].
