# Wind Power

A wind turbine turns the kinetic energy of moving air into electricity. The power in the wind
grows with the cube of wind speed, so a site with somewhat stronger wind gives much more energy.

Turbines have a cut-in speed below which they do not run and a cut-out speed above which they
stop to protect themselves.

Turbines at sea face stronger, steadier winds but cost more to build and maintain, see
[[Offshore Wind]]. Output varies with the weather, which lowers the [[Capacity Factor]].
