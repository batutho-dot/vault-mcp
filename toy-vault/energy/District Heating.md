# District Heating

District heating sends hot water through insulated pipes from central plants to many buildings.

Heat can come from combined heat and power plants, waste incineration, large heat pumps and
surplus heat from industry, including the waste heat from [[Electrolysis]].

Lower supply temperatures cut pipe losses and raise the efficiency of heat pumps; see the COP
section in [[Heat Pump#COP]].
