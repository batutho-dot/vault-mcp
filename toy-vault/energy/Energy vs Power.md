# Energy vs Power

Power is a rate: how fast energy is delivered. Its unit is the watt (W).

Energy is the amount delivered over time. Power multiplied by time gives energy, so a 1 kW
heater running for one hour uses 1 kWh.

A power plant is described by its rated power (for example in MW). What it actually produces in
a year is energy (MWh), which depends on its [[Capacity Factor]].

Unit conversions are in the [[Glossary of Units]].
