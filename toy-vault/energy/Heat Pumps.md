# Heat Pumps

A heat pump moves heat from a cold place to a warm place using electricity, the same way a
fridge does but in reverse.

## COP

Its efficiency is the coefficient of performance (COP): heat delivered divided by electricity
used. COP is above 1 because the pump moves heat rather than making it. It falls as the
temperature gap between source and sink grows.

Large heat pumps can feed [[District Heating]] networks.
