# Glossary of Units

- W (watt): unit of power, one joule per second.
- kWh (kilowatt hour): energy of 1 kW for one hour, equal to 3.6 MJ.
- MW, GW: a million and a billion watts.
- kg CO2-eq: climate impact expressed as the mass of CO2 with the same effect, see
  [[Global Warming Potential]].
