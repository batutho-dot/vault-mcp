# Electrolysis

Water electrolysis uses electricity to split water into hydrogen and oxygen.

Main technologies:

- Alkaline electrolysis: mature, uses a liquid electrolyte.
- Proton exchange membrane (PEM): responds fast to changing power.
- Solid oxide: runs hot and can use heat to lower electricity demand.

Part of the electricity ends up as heat, which can be used in [[District Heating]]. The climate
footprint of the hydrogen depends mostly on the electricity used, see [[Green Hydrogen]].
