# Power-to-X

Power-to-X (PtX) is the family of routes that turn electricity into other energy carriers or
chemicals. The first step is usually [[Electrolysis]] to make hydrogen.

The hydrogen can be used directly or combined with other molecules:

- with nitrogen to make ammonia,
- with carbon dioxide to make methanol or synthetic fuels.

The carbon dioxide has to come from somewhere, for example biogenic sources or
[[Carbon Capture and Storage]] chains. PtX is a candidate for sectors that are hard to
electrify directly, such as shipping and aviation.
