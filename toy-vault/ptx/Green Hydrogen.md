# Green Hydrogen

"Green" hydrogen is made by [[Electrolysis]] powered by renewable electricity. The colour names
are informal; a life cycle view is more precise, because the footprint depends on the actual
electricity supply and on the hours the electrolyser runs.

An electrolyser run on the average [[Electricity Grid Mix]] can have a larger footprint than one
run only when [[Wind Power]] is in surplus. That is why rules for renewable hydrogen look at
timing and location of the power.

Hydrogen is the first step in most [[Power-to-X]] chains.
